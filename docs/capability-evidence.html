01The trust boundary, in one request

The same message in three places. The provider is handed pseudonyms; the caller gets their own data back. This is the difference between a policy and a mechanism.

What the caller sent

My email is dana@corp.example and my card is 4111 1111 1111 1111.

What the provider received

My email is «12adcfb5142ef79a» and my card is «287a7978ea50c188».

What the caller got back

[echo:3fabb354] My email is dana@corp.example and my card is 4111 1111 1111 1111.

CARD, EMAIL were detected and replaced before dispatch. The provider never saw dana@corp.example or the card number — only reversible tokens. Restoration is scoped to the tenant that owns them.
