ONE REQUEST, VISIBLE DECISIONS

Follow the whole path.

Controls are useful when you can explain what they did.
Open the console to inspect the actual response and record.

  1. 01

    Check access

    Resolve the caller's tenant and scope. Enforce rate limits, token budgets and model permissions.

    Identity → policy
  2. 02

    Screen & mask

    Score supported injection signals and replace recognized personal-data patterns with reversible aliases.

    Input → guarded request
  3. 03

    Route & respond

    Call a configured provider, handle supported failure paths, and restore masked values in the response.

    Provider → application
  4. 04

    Record & verify

    Append a signed, linked audit entry. Verification checks sequence, signatures and payload digests.

    Decision → evidence
